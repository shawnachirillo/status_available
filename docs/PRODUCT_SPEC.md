# Product specification

STATUS: AVAILABLE provides curated customizable single-page websites for startups and small businesses. Open, Limited and Original are the Edition categories. Buying an Edition gives immediate access to a managed self-service editor. Preserve curated design constraints and the immutable purchased Edition.

## Approved requirements

- Twelve months of managed hosting, a free STATUS subdomain, and support for customer-owned custom domains.
- Email, telephone and external booking links.
- Opt-in hosting renewal with reminders 60, 30, 14 and 7 days before expiration.
- Private unpublished previews and shareable preview links expiring after seven days.
- Up to 250 MB and 50 images per website, with an individual upload limit of 10 MB.
- Static website export, managed handoff and documentation-first support.
- Thirty days of grace after hosting ends, followed by ninety days of protected retention. Active and grace permit public delivery and authorized editing, publishing, export and renewal. Suspension stops delivery, editing and publication while preserving data. Authorized hosting management and renewal remain available during protected retention.
- Refunds, revoked entitlements and invalid ownership override hosting eligibility. Retention expiration permits controlled deletion. Unattended permanent deletion requires an approved retention and recovery policy.

Prices, payment terms, Edition inventory quantities, domain registration commitments, and the retention and recovery policy remain unconfirmed. Approved requirements above do not imply implementation is complete.
